# LiPo Battery (Lithium Polymer)
## What it is
A LiPo battery is a rechargeable battery of lithium-ion technology using a polymer electrolyte instead of a liquid one. They are usually flat and soft.

## Real-world Example
Professional drones (quadcopters), RC planes, and some very thin modern laptops.

## How it works
Similar to Li-ion, but because they use a polymer (a type of plastic) inside, they can be made into almost any shape and are very thin and light. They can release their energy very, very quickly.

## Beginner Explanation (2nd-5th Class)
A LiPo battery is the "athlete" of the battery world. It's super light and can give a huge "burst" of energy to make a drone fly high or a racing robot go super fast. But because it's so powerful, it can be a bit grumpy and needs to be handled with great care.

## Intermediate Explanation (6th-10th Class)
LiPo batteries are rated by their "C" rating (Discharge Rate). For example, a 20C battery can release current at 20 times its capacity. They also have a nominal voltage of **3.7V per cell**. A "3S" LiPo has 3 cells in series, making it **11.1V**.

## Engineering Explanation (Diploma/Engineering)
LiPo batteries have a very low internal resistance, allowing for extremely high discharge currents ($>100A$ for some packs). However, they are chemically unstable if overcharged. They must be "Balanced" during charging so that each cell has exactly the same voltage.

## Safety Tips
- **CRITICAL:** Always charge LiPo batteries in a fireproof "LiPo Bag." Never leave them alone while charging.
- If a LiPo battery starts to look "puffy" or swollen like a marshmallow, stop using it immediately! It is dangerous and could catch fire.

## Common Mistakes
- Leaving a LiPo battery fully charged for a long time (more than a week). You should use the "Storage Charge" setting on your charger if you aren't using them.
- Shorting the wires together, which will cause an immediate and dangerous fire.

## Applications
- Racing drones.
- Radio-controlled (RC) cars and planes.
- Portable high-power medical devices.

## Simple Experiments
**Drone Power:** Look at a professional drone. See how the LiPo battery is mounted on the outside so it can stay cool and be easily replaced.

## FAQs
**Q: Why is my LiPo battery getting fat?**
A: That's called "puffing." It happens because of gas building up inside from old age or from being used too hard. It means the battery is now dangerous.
**Q: Can I use a normal battery charger?**
A: NO! You must use a dedicated LiPo Balance Charger.
